#### **0 What is missing in the D2 demo**

The docs say "Build yourself a "summer intern" in just 60 to 90 minutes with AIP! Extract the most important points and connections from your vast library of specialist literature, and start getting reliable answers to your questions." **I'd rather having a working demo of the core functionality... not a keyword search**.
 
Capital letters in Vertex create separate "entity" entries for the same word. Such functionality is the opposite of the core of what AI is. <br><img src="/assets/777_37.png" alt="drones" width="35%" style="border: 1px solid #999;">

Entities are keywords! They were selected by AI, but they are not embeddings (AI encoded meaning). Just text. 

No workflow diagrams (I mean detailed diagrams, that show the steps involved).

No heading numbering (or individual steps). Only step groups are numbered. (1,2,3)

Not updated/maintained. When setting up AIP, the following dialog magically appears. And its not explained in the docs (no screenshot). Anyone who actually does this demo will run into this. Its quite confusing when you run into it (no bit problem though... FDE to the rescue as always). <br><img src="/assets/777_38.png" alt="drones" width="45%" style="border: 1px solid #999;">

Foundry itself is awesome. As is FDE (simply magic). 
But the quality of these initial demos is not reassuring. 
I guess that doesn't matter ... PAL business is booming, and most of the on-site is done by FDE's. But in the future?

#### **1 Palantir protects enterprises from AI companies that steal secrets via prompts?**
Foundry manages access to all aspects of AI. But you are still using external AI, so you are still sending data to AI companies (but thats much better than if employees had uncontrolled access). If you really want to keep your data safe, you'd use local models (with RAG, which is demo'd in this demo). This is of course the case... what interesting is those interviewing Alex dont ask that or ask for him to explain just how Palantir protects data. I dont show this in demo 2 yet, but thats in my near term future plans... I will work on that part with GPT/FDE (AI assistants) directly.
