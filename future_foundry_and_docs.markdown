### **2 This is the future of programming Foundry (and writing documentation)**

In the (near) future 
- FDE "sees" your Foundry dialog (which has tight security controls, or you use an external camera that you can physically control for extra security). 
- You say (audio) what to do. Or maybe you point at the screen, or your PC can detect where exactly you are looking. 
- FDE types in and clicks in the dialogs.
- Programming speeds will be vastly faster, and the bar for programming productivity will be raised once again.

This will change our lives.
- I remember the first webpages. They were incredibly difficult to use, and most of us did not see much value in them. We were quite mistaken. 
- The tools eventually arrived for the non-expert to create websites.

The requirements for this to work:
- This will require massive levels of computing power. This will require
  - Efficient GPUs (a tech level monopoly like Nvidia has now)
  - Commodity LLMs (the LLMs will be secondary in importance; already the choice of LLM for FDE is not that important; we are not driving a car, just programming)
