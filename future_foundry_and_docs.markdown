I am writing the **[2.1 ZAI start-to-finish demo project](/3c.2_pal_initial_demo_ZAI/)** by simply chatting with FDE/GPT only. 
- Most of the text is FDE chat responses and the screenshots submitted to FDE (FDE can "see" screenshots and diagrams). 
- In the future, you will simply talk with FDE and FDE can see your screen. 
- Programming speeds will be vastly faster, and the bar for programming productivity will be raised once again.

Docx **[#695](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1-Adawag9uA8_bq-hDF-nOuPYaRLz1eEO)** is my working notes for the demo (mostly screenshots and chats with FDE/GPT). The most important part of learning Foundry is **learning how to use FDE or GPT (AI) to help you learn Foundry**.

Now FDE can "see" any dialogs/diagrams you paste into the chat. This is truly revolutionary. This is probably based on GPT tech (FDE probably provides the link to the current Foundry instance). 

FDE has NO intelligence. It is, however, an engineering marvel that points to the future of enterprise programming.


<br>

The future: FDE "sees" your Foundry dialog (which has tight security controls, or you use an external camera that you can physically control for extra security). 
- You say (voice) what to do.
- FDE types in and clicks in the dialogs.

This is the future new level of abstraction
- that is (not "will") changing the future.
- the QS for Foundry I am writing will focus on workflows and the prompts to submit to FDE. 

<br>

Notes:
- I worked 25 years ago at datango, a Berlin startup that had a product that was trying to do what I am doing now using FDE/GPT to help me program. datango's SW created popups inside a browser with instructions. But this was not AI, had to be programmed, and the startup idea failed (not sure what happened to the startup). FDE/GPT really works.
- This will require massive levels of computing power. This will require
  - Most efficient GPUs (a tech level monopoly like was Nvidia has now)
  - Commodity LLMs (the LLMs will be secondary... already the choice of LLM for FDE is not that important... we are not driving a car, just programming)
