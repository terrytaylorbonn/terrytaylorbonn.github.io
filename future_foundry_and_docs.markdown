### **0 How I started learning Foundry**

I spent a couple of months going through Foundry demos (see **["3.2 PAL project/doc sources"](/pal/)**). They were super helpful. But what I needed was 
- A personal trainer, an expert with Foundry.
- This was critical for fixing unexpected problems.
- Feedback on the accuracy of what I was doing. 
- Ability to change the demo focus, direction, level of complexity, etc.

### **2 This is the future of programming Foundry (and writing documentation)**

FDE does all of this with NO intelligence. It does not need intelligence (humans supply that). An improvement in FDE performance only requires better training and more powerful/efficient GPUs.

In the (near) future 
- FDE can directly "see" your Foundry instance dialogs (which has tight security controls, or you use an external camera that you can physically control for extra security). 
- To interact with FDE, you say (audio) what to do, point at the screen, or you simply look at the area of interest. 
- FDE makes the required changes directly.
- The bar for programming productivity will be raised once again.

This will require massive levels of computing power:
  - Efficient GPUs (a tech level monopoly like Nvidia has now)
  - Commodity LLMs (the LLMs will be secondary in importance; already the choice of LLM for FDE is not that important; we are not driving a car, just programming)

### **3 Such changes are the norm**

A few example of such changes 

- Such programming paradigm shifts have happened before. 
  - I remember the first webpages. They were incredibly difficult to use, and most of us did not see much value in them. We were quite mistaken. 
  - The tools eventually arrived for the non-expert to create websites. Its impossible to imagine life now without them.
- **I previously worked on a project** that was a failed attempt at creating such documentation. 25 years ago datango, a Berlin startup, had a product that was trying to do what I am doing now using FDE/GPT to help me program. datango's SW created popups inside a browser with instructions. But this was not AI, had to be programmed, and the startup idea failed (not sure what happened to the startup). 
- An experienced pilot talks about how he believes **[airliners are already "basically" flying themselves](https://youtu.be/fVI2BPsxLd0?t=1017)**.
