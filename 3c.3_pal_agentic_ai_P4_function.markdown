### Q1: Not yet bound to ontology

Correct. The Logic references Ontology resources but is **not yet formally bound to the Ontology**.

Before publishing:

1. Click **Select** beside **ziptieai Ontology**.
2. Confirm that it appears under **Bound to the following ontologies**.
3. Save.
4. Publish.

Binding makes the published Function available to Ontology-based consumers such as Automate and Workshop. It does not modify the Patient or Clinical Trial objects.
